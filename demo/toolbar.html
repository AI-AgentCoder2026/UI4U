ui-toolbar

A row of controls that behaves as one tab stop. Tab enters and leaves the bar in a single press; the arrow keys move between the controls inside it. Holds ui-input, ui-button, or anything else focusable — they stay light-DOM children and keep working on their own.

Press Tab once to enter the bar, then use the arrow keys.

A ui-toolbar can sit directly in any ui-layout region as a thin, non-resizable band — an app bar. It takes its height from its controls and never gets a splitter.

A ui-toolbar written before the <table> becomes that grid's own toolbar — the search, filters and bulk actions that belong to the table rather than to the page around it. Reach it through the toolbar getter.

Markup, API and the keyboard contract. Both blocks are copyable.
  • The bar carries role="toolbar" and is named by its label. An unlabelled toolbar is announced as an unnamed group of buttons.
  • Exactly one control holds tabindex="0". Tab therefore enters and leaves the whole bar in one press — which is the entire point of the component.
  • ArrowRight and ArrowLeft (or Up/Down when vertical) move between controls and emit ui4u:navigate. Movement clamps at the ends rather than wrapping.
  • Tab is never intercepted, so the bar is never a keyboard trap.
  • Disabled, hidden and aria-hidden controls are skipped by arrow navigation.
