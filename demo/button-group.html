ui-button-group

Several buttons that together make one choice — a segmented control. The segments stay real <ui-button>s; the group owns the selection, the single tab stop and the arrow-key model, and reports it all as one control to assistive technology.

Markup, options and theming. All three blocks are copyable.
  • The host is role="radiogroup" and each segment is role="radio" with aria-checked — several buttons acting as one choice, which is exactly what a segmented control is. The row wrapper is role="presentation", so the radios are owned by the group directly.
  • The label is a plain <span>, not a <label>: there is no single form control to point for at, and a label wrapping the buttons would swallow their clicks. It is associated through aria-labelledby, and sr-only clips it without taking the group's accessible name away.
  • The group is one tab stop: the selected segment, or the first enabled one, holds tabindex="0" and the rest hold -1.
  • Arrow keys move and select; Home and End jump to the ends. Movement clamps instead of wrapping and the key is swallowed even when nothing moved — otherwise Chromium's own handling would wrap the selection. Tab is never prevented, so it leaves the group.
  • Re-selecting the current segment keeps it selected: a choice, not a toggle. Disabled segments are skipped by the arrow keys, cannot be selected, and never hold the tab stop.
  • hint and error are chained through aria-describedby; an error sets aria-invalid and required sets aria-required. Every segment is at least 44px tall, so each is a comfortable touch target.

Which one should I use?

A ui-button-group is a choice between a handful of options that all stay visible — view mode, density, sort order. Reach for ui-radio-group when the options are a longer list that benefits from real form controls, and ui-tabs when they switch between panels rather than pick a value.
