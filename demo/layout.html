Markup, options and the public API.

Any slot can hold a ui-layout instead of a ui-panel, so a sidebar can be a workspace of its own — with its own rail, centre and bottom bar — to any depth. The example above nests three deep: an outer workspace, an explorer inside its left slot, and an inspector inside its right slot.

Each nested layout keeps its own separators, sizes and keyboard step, and resizing one does not disturb the others. Events still bubble, so every ui4u:resize carries the layout that emitted it.

  • ← / → — resize a side panel by the configured step
  • ↑ / ↓ — resize the top or bottom panel
  • Home — collapse the panel to its minimum
  • End — expand the panel to its maximum
  • Enter — collapse or restore the panel
  • Double-click — reset both adjacent panels to their authored size

Every separator is a role="separator" carrying aria-orientation — vertical for the ones between left/centre/right, horizontal for top/centre/bottom — plus aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin and aria-valuemax when a bound is enforced, so assistive tech announces the live size rather than leaving the drag an unlabelled gesture.
