Markup, options and the public API.
  • ← / → — resize by the configured step
  • Home — collapse the panel to its minimum
  • End — expand the panel to its maximum
  • Enter — collapse or restore the panel
  • Double-click — reset both adjacent panels to their authored size

Each separator is a role="separator" with aria-valuenow, aria-valuemin and aria-valuemax, so assistive tech announces the live size rather than leaving the drag as an unlabelled gesture.
