ui-sidebar

A navigation landmark with roving arrow-key focus, a collapse toggle, a rail mode that expands on focus, and an off-canvas drawer below 48rem. Try ↓ ↑, Home and End.

Components

Items are authored as light DOM. ui-sidebar renders the real <a href> or <button> inside each <ui-sidebar-item> and owns every role, aria-* attribute and tab stop, so the two can never disagree.

Rail mode

Collapsed, the sidebar becomes a narrow rail. Focus entering a collapsed rail expands it again, and every control keeps its label as a title — so a bare glyph is never the only thing a keyboard or screen reader user has.

Keyboard

↑ ↓ move between items on the left, ← → on the right, Home and End jump to the ends, and Enter or Space activates. Only the active item is in the tab order.
