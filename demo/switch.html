ui-switch

A binary setting that applies the moment it is flipped. That is the whole difference from ui-checkbox: a checkbox is part of a form submission and needs a real input with a value to send, while a switch has no form value to preserve — so it is a real <button role="switch">, and a rejected change has nothing to undo.

A settings row

The shape a switch actually appears in: a list of settings that each take effect at once. The label sits beside the control rather than around it, and carries the click itself, so the whole row is one comfortable target on a phone.

More

Markup, options and theming. All three blocks are copyable.
  • The control is a genuine <button role="switch"> with aria-checked, so Enter and Space, :focus-visible, :disabled and every screen reader come from the platform.
  • The track is drawn with aria-hidden="true" and the state is read from aria-checked, so the painted switch and the announced switch cannot disagree.
  • The label is a <span> beside the control, wired through aria-labelledby. A <label> that wraps another control swallows its clicks, so it never does — the row handler takes the click instead.
  • hint and error are chained through aria-describedby; an error sets aria-invalid, and required sets aria-required.
  • Every control is at least 44px tall at every size — the track scales with size, the touch target does not.
  • The only motion is the knob and the track colour, both expressed through --ui4u-duration-* and --ui4u-ease-*, which the token layer collapses to 0ms under prefers-reduced-motion.
