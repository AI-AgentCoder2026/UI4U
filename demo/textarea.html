ui-textarea

A multiline field that wraps a real <textarea>. Native spellcheck, autocorrect, IME composition and mobile keyboards keep working because the control is never re-rendered from a property.

Type, then press Tab or Ctrl+Enter to commit.

Add grow and the field expands to fit what you wrote instead of scrolling inside itself. It only ever grows while you are typing — deleting text never yanks the box in under your cursor — and tightens back up once you leave the field. Past --ui4u-textarea-grow-max it scrolls like any other field.
