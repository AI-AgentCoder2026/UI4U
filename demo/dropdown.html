This component follows the WAI-ARIA select-only combobox pattern: the trigger is a real <button> and it keeps focus for the entire interaction.

  • The trigger carries aria-haspopup="listbox", aria-expanded and aria-controls pointing at the panel; the panel is role="listbox" and carries aria-multiselectable when multiple is set, each option is role="option" with aria-selected, and disabled options carry aria-disabled.
  • The keyboard cursor is published with aria-activedescendant, so a screen reader follows the options without focus ever leaving the trigger. That is also why the portalled panel needs no focus trap.
  • Closed: Enter/Space/↓/↑ open, Home/End open onto the first/last option, typing jumps to a matching label. Open: ↑/↓ move, Home/End jump, Enter commits, Space commits or toggles when multiple, Esc closes and returns focus to the trigger, and Tab closes without swallowing the key.
  • Disabled options are skipped by arrow navigation and type-ahead, and can never be committed by click or keyboard.
  • The trigger's text content is the current selection, so the value is part of its accessible name. With sr-only the visible label is clipped but still read.
