ui-popup

A modal, a popover and a drawer from one component. Open the modal and press Tab repeatedly — focus stays inside. Esc closes it.

Markup, modes and the event surface.

In modal mode focus is trapped: Tab and Shift+Tab cycle within the dialog, and focus cannot be pulled out by clicking the backdrop. Closing returns focus to whatever was focused beforehand — usually the button that opened it.

popover and drawer deliberately do not trap focus; a drawer is a side panel you carry on working with. Only modal sets aria-modal="true".

The surface is portalled to <body>. Without that, an ancestor with a backdrop-filter or transform would become the containing block for its position: fixed coordinates and the dialog would open somewhere unrelated.
